# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-led community. Be constructive,
assume good faith, discuss system mechanisms rather than assigning blame, and
respect privacy and confidentiality.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private incident
details, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may
edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation to
protect the project and its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile contact channel. Include links or screenshots when safe. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and
the subject of a report excluded from its resolution.

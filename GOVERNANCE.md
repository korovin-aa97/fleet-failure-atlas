# Governance

Fleet Failure Atlas is a maintainer-led open-source project. Alexander Korovin is
the initial maintainer and release manager.

Maintainers curate scope, assign stable IDs, review safety and provenance,
approve changes, and publish releases. Technical decisions prefer deterministic
evidence, safe execution, backward-compatible pattern IDs, and a small coherent
catalogue. Decisions and rationale should be recorded in public issues or pull
requests unless doing so would disclose a security or privacy concern.

Substantial contributors may be invited as maintainers after sustained,
high-quality review and pattern work. Maintainers must disclose relevant
conflicts, recuse when necessary, and follow the code of conduct.

If maintenance stops, the maintainer may archive the project or transfer it to a
trusted contributor who commits to its safety and provenance standards.
